SYSTEM OVERVIEW
===============
The system is a Role-based Access Control (RBAC) environment supporting multiple users, role hierarchies, temporary role delegation, revocation, and concurrent request processing. The system enforces authorization correctness, maintains delegation and permission consistency, and ensures safety and liveness under concurrency. Users have clearance levels (High, Medium, Low), and objects have classification levels (High, Medium, Low). Requests are classified by the highest classification level of affected objects and must comply with user clearance. Higher-clearance users can approve or reject lower-clearance user requests. Delegation and revocation of roles are governed by clearance and administrative rules. The system includes an Emergency Module to manage exceptional scenarios, which alters standard behaviors under specific triggers and constraints. Emergency mode is abstracted as a fixed duration but may terminate early if both administrators who activated it jointly deactivate it.

EXISTING-SYSTEM ASSUMPTIONS AND CONSTRAINTS
============================================
- Emergency mode can only be triggered from Normal mode and requires exactly two administrators acting jointly.
- Emergency mode is time-bounded, abstracted as a fixed duration, but may terminate early if both participating administrators jointly deactivate Emergency; the system must eventually return to Normal mode.
- FIFO ordering for request processing can be bypassed only during Emergency mode, and only for high-classification requests.
- Mutual exclusivity of roles is suspended only during Emergency mode.
- Emergency privileges are held only by the two administrators who triggered Emergency mode, only during Emergency mode, and must be revoked after.
- Roles delegated during Emergency mode must be revoked when Emergency mode ends. Roles delegated prior to Emergency mode are not automatically revoked, but may still expire or be revoked by an administrator during Emergency mode.
- Only the administrators who participated in the Emergency trigger are required to update their credentials after Emergency mode ends.

PROSPECTIVE FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
====================================
R1: Emergency Trigger  
Exactly two administrators, while the system is in Normal mode, are required to jointly trigger entry into Emergency mode. Only these two participating administrators are considered for all subsequent emergency-related requirements.

R2: Emergency Mode Duration  
Emergency mode is active only for a limited (bounded) time, abstracted as a fixed duration, but may terminate early if both participating administrators jointly deactivate Emergency. The system must eventually return to Normal mode.

R3: Emergency Mode Behavior  
R3.1. During Emergency mode, request processing may bypass FIFO order to prioritize and process multiple high-classification requests simultaneously.  
R3.2. During Emergency mode, users may temporarily hold roles that are normally mutually exclusive.  
R3.3. During Emergency mode, only the administrators who participated in the Emergency trigger temporarily hold emergency privileges.

R4: Post-Emergency Restoration  
R4.1. After Emergency mode ends, emergency privileges must be revoked from only the administrators who participated in the Emergency trigger.  
R4.2. After Emergency mode ends, any roles delegated during Emergency mode must be revoked. Delegated roles from before Emergency mode remain subject to normal expiration or administrator revocation, including during Emergency mode.  
R4.3. After Emergency mode ends, only the administrators who participated in the Emergency trigger must update their credentials.
