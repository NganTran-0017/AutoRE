SYSTEM DESCRIPTION:
The Role-Based Access Control system operates in a multi-user environment with High, Medium, and Low security clearances and supports clearance-constrained role assignment, mutually exclusive role enforcement, temporary role delegation, administrator-controlled revocation, request submission, approval, rejection, and concurrent request processing. The system must preserve authorization correctness, delegation and permission consistency, auditable role and request histories, liveness of request processing, and safety under concurrent operation.

Existing requirements:
E1. Each user in the system is assigned a security clearance level: High, Medium, or Low. 
    E1.1. Administrators have High clearance level, but not all High-clearance users are Administrators. 
E2. Users may hold roles via direct assignment and delegated roles, subject to clearance constraints. 
    E2.1. Users may not hold or be assigned roles that are multually exclusive to each other during Normal operation. 
    E2.2. Users may not hold, be assigned or delegated with roles that are higher than their clearance level. 
E3. Users may temporarily delegate their assigned roles to another user with lower clearance level, provided that the delegate meets the role’s minimum clearance requirement. 
    E3.1. Delegated roles must expire after a fixed amount of time or be revoked at any time by an administrator. All delegation record, including the delegator, delegate, delegated role, initiated time, delegated role's expiration or early revocation and by whom, must be auditable. 
    E3.2. No self-delegation is allowed. 
E4. Users may submit requests for actions.
    E4.1. Each request for an action can affect one or more system objects, each with a classification level of High, Medium, or Low. The classification of a request is the highest classification of all affected objects. 
    E4.2. Users may submit requests which have classification levels less than or equal to the user's clearance level. 
    E4.3. Users with strictly higher clearance may approve or reject requests from lower-clearance users. 
    E4.4. Requests are submitted and processed concurrently in FIFO order—meaning no newer request is processed before its predecessors—and every request is guaranteed to be eventually processed. All request processing record, including the request status, request's classification level and request's processor, must be auditable.

REQUIREMENTS FOR NEW MODULE: Emergency Module

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

Emergency module requirements:
R1. While operating in Normal mode, exactly 2 administrators are required to trigger an emergency, causing the system to enter the Emergency mode. The system can only enter the Emergency mode once.
R2. The Emergency mode is active only for a limited time, and the system must eventually return to Normal mode.
R3. During the Emergency mode:
    R3.1. Request processing may bypass FIFO order to prioritize handling multiple higher-classification requests concurrently. Requests are prioritized by their classification level, ensuring higher-classification requests are always processed before lower-classification ones. 
    R3.2. Users may temporarily hold mutual exclusive roles. 
    R3.3. Administrators, who trigger the Emergency mode, may temporarily hold emergency privileges.
R4. After Emergency mode ends:
    R4.1. Emergency privileges must be revoked from the administrators.
    R4.2. Delegated roles initiated during Emergency must be revoked. Delegated roles initiated during Normal mode, prior to Emergency mode, can persists until its expiration, unless it is revoked early by an administrator.
    R4.3. Administrators must update their credentials.
